
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
All contributors should be respectful, professional, and supportive. Harassment and discrimination are not acceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions.

## Enforcement
Violations may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers may issue warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans depending on the severity of the violation.
